Everything you need to practice with purpose

TrajectoryX makes those numbers work for you: a range you read at a glance, your real shot pattern per club, games and drills that score every ball, and swing videos with the computed flight drawn in — two swings side by side if you like — on iPhone and iPad.

My Bubble New in 8.5

Know where your ball really lands

Set the flag to a club's carry and hit 8+ balls at it. My Bubble shows where your average ball lands — "Average ball: 3 yd short of the flag, 5 yd right" — and your spread: how far long, short, left and right you actually miss. 20+ balls make the bubble solid, and every club gets its own.

  • My BubbleYour shot pattern per club, from the balls you hit at the flag
  • Aim PlannerSlide the green against your bubble and read the live "% on the green"
  • Put it to workIn the Aim Planner you slide the green sideways against your pattern — "Aim 9 yd left of the flag · 89 % on the green" — and keep swinging straight at the range's center line. The next balls show honestly whether the plan holds: "Aim plan 11 yd left: 7 of 8 on the green".

Any tile can be a game

Give a tile a lower and upper limit. A shot outside turns the limit red and sounds different, so you know without looking; an orange badge counts your shots in a row inside, a green one your best run of the session. Then pick a game:

  • X ShotsYour target of hits, in as few shots as you can

  • TimedBeat the clock
  • Combine tilesA shot only counts when all of them are inside — or as many as you choose.
  • Records that lastPersonal records carry over from session to session; a ★ on the green badge marks a new one.

Read every shot at a glance

Every number the moment you hit, beside the flight the app computes from your launch monitor's data — laid out the way you like.

Range mode

Ball, spin and club data appear as you hit, next to the flight the app computes from them. Flip between a top view with a dispersion ellipse per club, a height profile, diagrams of club path, face and attack angle, the 3D range, your tiles and the shot Table — or split the screen in two; the app remembers your layout. On a GCQuad or QuadMAX with club data, the Impact tab maps where each shot struck the face.

Your tiles, your layout

Long-press to reorder or hide tiles, pinch for one to three columns, and double-tap one to read it big from the mat. Every tile shows its session average and spread. And each launch monitor shows only the numbers it really delivers — no empty face-angle tiles on a unit that can't see the face.

Every shot in one table

The Table tab lists every shot of the session with average and standard deviation per club, and names the ball flight model and roll behind each total. Tap a row to see that shot, untick a mishit and it drops out of the stats, tap the club cell to fix a wrong club, double-tap for fullscreen — and export to CSV or Excel.

Carry, three ways

No more guessing which carry a tile means: Carry Distance runs along the target line, Carry Offline to the side, Carry Diagonal straight to the ball — the length a longest-drive contest measures. Total comes the same three ways. The Diagonal tiles start hidden — add them with +.

Flag, green — and a score for every shot

Put a flag and green on the range: drag to set the distance, pinch the green into a wide or deep ellipse, like the greens you play to. With a flag out, every shot gets a TM Score from 0 to 100 and strokes gained against that distance — and the same flag is where My Bubble counts your balls.

Your conditions, your units

Set temperature, humidity, elevation, tee-to-green height difference, wind and turf firmness — and, for the roll, the landing surface, green speed and landing slope — and the computed flight follows. Pick yards or meters, mph, km/h or m/s, °F or °C, and L/R or signed numbers for left and right.

Three ball flight models New in 8.5

TrajectoryX Default is free and designed in-house. Trajectory Model+ flies the ball like Foresight's app — and new in 8.5, Trajectory Model TM flies and rolls like TrackMan's. The roll is a choice of its own — TrajectoryX, Foresight-like or TrackMan-like, with any model; the Foresight-like and TrackMan-like rolls read the landing ground you set per environment: Landing Surface, Stimp and Landing Slope. Model+, Model TM and the Foresight-like and TrackMan-like rolls are part of Pro and share 10 free shots a day.

Forgot to switch the club? New in 8.5

With a launch monitor, the app notices when your numbers don't fit the selected club — "Driver selected? Your first 2 shots look like an iron." — and asks. One tap picks the right club and relabels those shots, "Keep" confirms them. It never changes a club on its own, and you can switch the check off in Settings › Club Check.

Connect without the guesswork

Setting up is guided, a GCQuad or GC3 connects over Bluetooth too, and when something goes wrong the app tells you why. Every monitor in detail under Devices below.

Guided connection

Pick your monitor and the app walks you through the steps, joining the unit's Wi-Fi for you where there is one. Next time it's already selected — just tap Continue. While connected, you see battery, device temperature and whether your Foresight unit sends full ball-and-club data or ball only.

GCQuad and GC3 over Bluetooth

Connect a Foresight GCQuad or GC3 over Bluetooth instead of its Wi-Fi hotspot — your phone stays on your normal network and keeps its internet while you practice. Several units in the room? The app lists them by serial number and remembers yours.

When it won't connect, it tells you why

Wrong Wi-Fi, no Wi-Fi, Local Network blocked, wrong monitor model — the app names the cause and the fix, and a shareable connection log helps support if it ever gets stuck. A Foresight unit's expiring offline license is flagged days before the monitor locks itself.

Know when the monitor is ready

A big orange notice tells you when a Foresight monitor is waiting — several balls in view, or the ball outside the hitting zone — and disappears the moment it's ready. If the link drops, the app notices within seconds and reconnects on its own when it can.

Challenges

Turn any data tile into a game

Put a corridor on a tile — launch angle 25 to 32°, carry within five yards, spin under 7000 — and pick the game you want to play with it. The tile counts for you while you hit, right on the range, and your personal records carry over from session to session:

Streak — how many in a row? +

Every shot inside the corridor extends your streak; the first one outside ends it. The tile shows the streak you are on and the best one of the session next to it, so you always know what you have to beat.

X Shots — reach the target, in as few as you can +

Set a goal of hits — say eight inside the corridor — and work towards it. The tile counts your hits and the shots you needed for them. Get there in fewer shots than last time and you have a new record.

Record — chase your extreme +

Go for your highest value, or your lowest: fastest ball speed, steepest launch, most spin on a wedge, least side spin. The tile remembers your personal best and tells you when you beat it.

Timed — beat the clock +

Choose a time, for example sixty seconds, and see how many shots you can land inside the corridor before it runs out. The clock starts on your first counted shot, so you can take your stance in peace.

Combine tiles into one challenge +

A single number rarely describes a good shot. Pick several tiles — launch angle and carry side, say — give each one its corridor, and a shot only counts when every one of them is inside. Or ask for a majority instead: any two of three. A value your launch monitor could not measure never counts against you.

Offline tiles: one “Max” limit New in 8.5 +

Carry Offline and Total Offline measure how far you miss sideways, so since 8.5 they take a single limit instead of a lower and an upper one. “± 10” means within 10 yards — or meters — left or right of the target line, and the tile's bar runs symmetrically from −10 to +10. A lower limit you had stored is dropped on its own.

Drills

Train the right way

Pick a predefined drill — or build your own Skill Games with the drill builder. Five Precision Drill types, plus the games you make yourself; every drill now has the same tabs as the range, including its own Data tab:

Trackman Combine & Shots Gained +

The classic Combine test. For every club except the driver you get a target and try to land your carry as close to it as possible — the closer you are, the higher the score. For the driver, hit as long (total distance) and as straight as possible.

On top of the Trackman score, the app shows the shots you gained (positive) or lost (negative) compared to the average PGA Tour player.

Spin +

Produce stopping power. Each spin drill defines a landing corridor; land inside it and your score scales linearly with the total spin you generate per meter/yard. Hit the target spin and you score 100 — double it and you score 200.

Optionally penalize long coasting to reward true "stopping power", with configurable thresholds for negative scoring.

Target Circle +

Great for pitch training. Two circles define a score of 100 (e.g. 2 yd) and 0 (e.g. 11 yd) around target distances. Between them the score is linear; inside the inner circle it keeps climbing above 100.

As Few Strokes As Possible +

A progression variant of the target-circle drill. Land in the circle and advance to the next distance; miss and you choose what happens — retry, drop back, or restart. The goal: finish all distances in as few shots as possible.

Ladder +

Climb a ladder of distances, up or down, from a start to an end distance. Land a valid shot on each rung before you move on to the next — within the retries you allow, per rung or for the whole ladder.

Build your own — Skill Games & Precision Drills +

The drill builder, since 8.4: start from one of five Skill Game templates or from scratch: draw target, bonus and penalty zones over your own shots, add rules on ball and club data, choose one of six ways to win, give it a name — done. Your Skill Games sit above the Precision Drills in the Drills tab, with the same Drill, Data and Table tabs, a BEST badge and a records list per game.

Prefer a Precision Drill? Create one of the five types above with your own distances, rounds, unit and scoring.

Everything else stays free — Pro is entirely optional. For the ball flight models the free in-house alternative is just as accurate for everyday practice; swing video beyond the 5 daily recordings is Pro only.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does TrajectoryX cost? +

All core features are free, including a high-quality ball flight model designed in-house, the Skill Games you build yourself and the ball tracer. Pro adds the Foresight-like and TrackMan-like ball flight models with their roll models — where the free in-house model is a full everyday replacement — unlimited swing video, which has no free equivalent beyond its daily allowance, 20× range zoom and corridors on the side-angle, side-spin, total-spin, spin-axis and club-data tiles. You can try the ball flight models and swing video every day for free (10 shots with the Pro models, 5 swing videos) and unlock everything with an optional subscription. My Bubble, the Aim Planner, the new swing player and Swing Compare are free.

Which launch monitors are supported? +

TrajectoryX works with the Foresight Sports GCQuad, QuadMAX, GC3 and GC3S, the Bushnell Launch Pro — which shares the GC3 connection — the TrackMan 4 and the Full Swing KIT, as well as the Garmin Approach R10 via the E6 Connect protocol and DIY units running OpenFlight. Support for the fixed-install Foresight GCHawk and Falcon is experimental for now. The app connects directly to your launch monitor — over Wi-Fi through the unit's own hotspot or your local network, or over Bluetooth for the Foresight GCQuad and GC3 family and the Full Swing KIT, which leaves your iPhone on its normal network.

Do I need an internet connection? +

No — your practice works fully offline and all your data is stored on your device. An internet connection is only needed for online competitions and leaderboards, and for the optional AI Coach.

My launch monitor won't connect — what now? +

The Connect screen walks you through it: power the unit on, join its Wi-Fi (the app can do that for you), and the handshake runs on its own. If the unit can't be reached, the app tells you why instead of just failing — your device is on a different Wi-Fi (iPads like to hop back to a network with internet), it isn't on any Wi-Fi at all, or iOS has Local Network access switched off for the app — each with the one thing to do next. A shot that arrives but isn't recorded, say into a session opened read-only, shows a short notice saying so. And if it still won't work, Connect → Show connection log → share sends us exactly what happened: info@TrajectoryX.com.

Is my data private? +

Yes. Everything is saved on-device by default and nothing is sent to any server. The only exception is the optional AI Coach: when you enable it and add your own API key, your shot data is sent to the AI provider you chose. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How does the AI Coach work? +

The AI Coach analyzes your session and gives you an overall grade, your strengths, what to improve and a targeted drill — picked from the app's own drills, so you can start it right away. You bring your own API key for Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini and choose the provider and model — your key stays on your device. The AI Coach is optional and stays off until you add a key; it isn't available in mainland China.

What devices does TrajectoryX run on? +

TrajectoryX is a native app for iPhone and iPad. The layout adapts to both, with extra data shown on larger screens and in landscape.

How does swing video work? +

Hit your shot and TrajectoryX records your swing on its own, then plays it back in slow motion — it detects the key swing positions (Address, Top, Impact, End) so you can jump straight to them and step through frame by frame. Practice swings that didn't hit a ball are flagged automatically, and when a launch monitor measured the shot, the app can trace the ball's flight into the video. No launch monitor required, and it pairs perfectly with one.

Since 8.5 the player shows the video edge to edge, upright or turned, with the positions as tabs and the tools — Speed, Loop, Trim, Tracer, Draw, Points — on a card below the picture. Swing Compare puts a second shot of yours next to the first: both move in step through the swing, and the data points below read A, B and B − A.

Ball-line and swing-plane guides on the video and multi-angle recording with several devices are planned for a later update.

What is the ball tracer in my swing video? +

The flight of the ball, drawn into your own video the way TV coverage traces a drive. It is not a guess from the footage: the curve is the flight the app computes from your launch monitor's data for that exact shot, laid over the frame and timed to the moment of impact — so the ball leaves the picture and the line carries on to where the app computes it to land.

Tap the tracer button in the video player. It appears whenever the clip belongs to a shot your launch monitor recorded.

Does swing video work indoors and outdoors? +

Outdoors it works great — with or without a launch monitor. Indoors, we recommend using a launch monitor: it tells the app exactly when you strike the ball, so every swing is captured cleanly. Recording indoors without a launch monitor is still experimental today and keeps getting better with every update.

Can I move my sessions to another device? +

Yes — you can export a whole practice session, including every shot and its swing videos, as a single file and import it on another iPhone or iPad. It's ideal for backing up your best sessions, switching devices or sharing a session with your coach — no cloud account needed.

What are tile challenges? +

Every data tile on the range can carry a corridor — a lower and an upper limit for that value, or a single “Max” limit on the Carry Offline and Total Offline tiles since 8.5. Since 8.4 a tile with a corridor can also play a game: count your streak inside it, work towards a target number of hits in as few shots as you can, chase your highest or lowest value, or land as many shots as possible before a clock runs out. You can combine several tiles into one challenge, so that a shot only counts when every value is inside its limits, and your personal records are kept across sessions.

What are My Bubble and the Aim Planner? +

My Bubble is your real shot pattern per club. Set the flag to a club's carry and hit 8+ balls at it; the app shows where your average ball lands — say 3 yards short of the flag and 5 right — and how far long, short, left and right you miss. 20+ balls make the bubble solid, and moving the flag starts a new count. The Aim Planner puts the bubble to work: slide the green sideways against your pattern and read the live “% on the green”, then keep swinging at the range's center line — the next balls show whether the plan holds. Both are in the range's ••• menu and free.

What is the 3D Range? +

The 3D Range is a new way to see your shot. Pick the "3D" view on the range and TrajectoryX replays your ball flight in a full 3D driving range, built from the flight the app computes from your launch monitor's data. Follow the ball from behind, down the target line or from overhead — or take the free camera and look from anywhere — with a live tracer and a shadow path on the ground. Pinch to zoom, drag to look around and double-tap for full screen. In Settings you choose the scene (mountains or ocean, comic or realistic) and the sky.
