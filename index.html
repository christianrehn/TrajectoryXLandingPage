Features

Everything you need to practice with purpose

From the range to the wedge game to global competitions — all driven by your launch monitor's real ball and club data.

Range mode

Set up your environment — temperature, humidity, elevation, units — and see ball, club and trajectory data for every shot, with 2D carry and dispersion. Online competitions

Compatibility

Works with your launch monitor

TrajectoryX connects directly over Wi-Fi and turns raw ball and club measurements into a full training experience.

Foresight Sports GCQuad / QuadMAX

Native, encrypted connection. Full ball, club and spin data is read in real time and used to calculate and display the ball flight for every shot. While you're connected, the unit's battery and temperature are shown at a glance — for the whole Foresight family.

Foresight GC3, GC3S & Bushnell Launch Pro

Connect your Foresight Sports GC3 or GC3S — or the Bushnell Launch Pro, which shares the very same connection — and get the full TrajectoryX experience: live ball, club and spin data on the range, just like the GCQuad and QuadMAX. New: connect over Bluetooth instead of the unit's Wi-Fi hotspot, and your iPhone stays on your normal network with its internet intact.

TrackMan 4 New

Connect your TrackMan 4 directly over Wi-Fi — no simulator software in between. You get live shot data, an instant flight preview about a second after impact, swing videos straight from the TrackMan camera, an aim dialog with the device's own camera view, and a view-only mode for when someone else is running the unit.

Garmin Approach R10 E6 Connect

Connect your Garmin Approach R10 through the E6 Connect protocol and use the same range, drill and competition features — no extra hardware required.

Foresight GCHawk & Falcon Experimental

These fixed-install units speak the same Foresight protocol, so TrajectoryX can connect to them too. Support is experimental for now — give it a try and let us know how it goes.

OpenFlight New

Built your own launch monitor with the open-source OpenFlight project? TrajectoryX speaks its protocol too — point the app at your device and every shot lands in the same range, drills and competitions as any commercial unit.

Drills

Train the right way

Pick a predefined drill — or build your own Skill Games with the new drill builder. Four drill types power them all, and every drill now has the same tabs as the range, including its own Data tab:

Trackman Combine & Shots Gained +

The classic Combine test. For every club except the driver you get a target and try to land your carry as close to it as possible — the closer you are, the higher the score. For the driver, hit as long (total distance) and as straight as possible.

On top of the Trackman score, the app shows the shots you gained (positive) or lost (negative) compared to the average PGA Tour player.

Spin +

Produce stopping power. Each spin drill defines a landing corridor; land inside it and your score scales linearly with the total spin you generate per meter/yard. Hit the target spin and you score 100 — double it and you score 200.

Optionally penalize long coasting to reward true "stopping power", with configurable thresholds for negative scoring.

Target Circle +

Great for pitch training. Two circles define a score of 100 (e.g. 2 yd) and 0 (e.g. 11 yd) around target distances. Between them the score is linear; inside the inner circle it keeps climbing above 100.

As Few Strokes As Possible +

A progression variant of the target-circle drill. Land in the circle and advance to the next distance; miss and you choose what happens — retry, drop back, or restart. The goal: finish all distances in as few shots as possible.

Build your own — Skill Games & Precision Drills +

New in 8.4: the drill builder. Start from a template or from scratch, pick one of the four types, set your distances, targets and scoring, give it a name — done. Your own drills line up right next to the predefined ones, with the same shot views, Data tab and score history.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does TrajectoryX cost? +

All core features are free, including a high-quality ball flight model designed in-house. Two extras are Pro: the Foresight Sports ball flight model — where the free in-house model is a full everyday replacement — and swing video, which has no free equivalent beyond its daily allowance. You can try both every day for free (5 shots with the Foresight model, 3 swing videos) and unlock them fully with an optional subscription.

Which launch monitors are supported? +

TrajectoryX works with the Foresight Sports GCQuad, QuadMAX, GC3 and GC3S, the Bushnell Launch Pro — which shares the GC3 connection — and the TrackMan 4, as well as the Garmin Approach R10 via the E6 Connect protocol and DIY units running OpenFlight. Support for the fixed-install Foresight GCHawk and Falcon is experimental for now. The app connects directly to your launch monitor — over Wi-Fi through the unit's own hotspot or your local network, and for the GC3 family now over Bluetooth as well, which leaves your iPhone on its normal network.

Do I need an internet connection? +

No — your practice works fully offline and all your data is stored on your device. An internet connection is only needed for online competitions and leaderboards, and for the optional AI Coach.

Is my data private? +

Yes. Everything is saved on-device by default and nothing is sent to any server. The only exception is the optional AI Coach: when you enable it and add your own API key, your shot data is sent to the AI provider you chose. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How does the AI Coach work? +

The AI Coach analyzes your session and gives you an overall grade, your strengths, what to improve and a targeted drill — picked from the app's own drills, so you can start it right away. You bring your own API key for Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini and choose the provider and model — your key stays on your device.

What devices does TrajectoryX run on? +

TrajectoryX is a native app for iPhone and iPad. The layout adapts to both, with extra data shown on larger screens and in landscape.

How does swing video work? +

Hit your shot and TrajectoryX records your swing on its own, then plays it back in slow motion — it detects the key swing points (Start, Top, Impact, End) so you can jump straight to them and step through frame by frame. Practice swings that didn't hit a ball are flagged automatically, and when a launch monitor measured the shot, the app can trace the ball's flight into the video. No launch monitor required, and it pairs perfectly with one.

Ball-line and swing-plane guides on the video and multi-angle recording with several devices are planned for a later update.

What is the ball tracer in my swing video? +

The flight of the ball, drawn into your own video the way TV coverage traces a drive. It is not a guess from the footage: the curve is the trajectory your launch monitor measured for that exact shot, laid over the frame and timed to the moment of impact — so the ball leaves the picture and the line carries on to where it actually landed.

Tap the tracer button in the video player. It appears whenever the clip belongs to a shot your launch monitor recorded.

Does swing video work indoors and outdoors? +

Outdoors it works great — with or without a launch monitor. Indoors, we recommend using a launch monitor: it tells the app exactly when you strike the ball, so every swing is captured cleanly. Recording indoors without a launch monitor is still experimental today and keeps getting better with every update.

Can I move my sessions to another device? +

Yes — you can export a whole practice session, including every shot and its swing videos, as a single file and import it on another iPhone or iPad. It's ideal for backing up your best sessions, switching devices or sharing a session with your coach — no cloud account needed.

What is the 3D Range? +

The 3D Range is a new way to see your shot. Pick the "3D" view on the range and TrajectoryX replays your ball flight in a full 3D driving range, built from the real trajectory measured by your launch monitor. Follow the ball from behind, down the target line or from overhead — or take the free camera and look from anywhere — with a live tracer and a shadow path on the ground. Pinch to zoom, drag to look around and double-tap for full screen. In Settings you choose the scene (mountains or ocean, comic or realistic) and the sky.
